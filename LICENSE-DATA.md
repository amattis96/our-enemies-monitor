To the extent that they own the applicable rights, the authors and contributors
of Our Enemies Monitor dedicate their original research content, project-created
datasets and compilations, schemas, metadata, annotations, and documentary
analyses to the public domain under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal.

This dedication applies, to the extent of rights held by the project's
contributors, to project-original material in these locations:

- `sources/original/`: original project-authored analytical text and
  project-created structured records, but **not** embedded third-party works.
- `data/`: original normalized records, assertions, relationships,
  evidence metadata, hypotheses and derivative project datasets.
- `research/`: original project-produced research reports and notes.
- `locales/`: original project-owned translations and explanatory content.
- `docs/` and Markdown documentation, including `README.md`, except for
  third-party material and software documentation governed by a separate license.

CC0 is intended to permit copying, mirroring, remixing, extracting, translating,
redistributing, publishing, and commercial or non-commercial reuse without
permission or mandatory attribution. Source citation and provenance retention
are **strongly encouraged for research integrity**, but are not added as
license conditions to material validly dedicated under CC0.

## Critical exclusions

This declaration does **not** dedicate to the public domain, license, or
override any rights in third-party materials, including external news articles,
photographs, official documents, trademarks, externally authored passages,
geographic basemaps or licensed external datasets.

The presence of a URL, quotation, paraphrase, research citation, archived
snapshot, embedded excerpt or reference to an external work in this repository
does not mean the external work is licensed under CC0. Reusers must review and
comply with the applicable rights and terms of each external source.

When original materials are contributed by others, contributors should only
make CC0 commitments for material they have the authority to dedicate. Mark
exceptions and externally licensed components explicitly, and preserve
required source attributions and license notices.

The project provides its data as-is, without any warranty of accuracy or
completeness. The CC0 dedication does not eliminate applicable privacy,

and content under these terms. The project encourages independent archival
copies and transparent fork provenance. An independent instance should not
falsely claim official endorsement or obscure substantive modifications.

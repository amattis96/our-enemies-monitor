# Translation glossary: evidence and legal status

Use the source and context to preserve the degree of certainty and procedural
status. Grammatical variants are valid; this is editorial guidance, not a
literal word-matching rule or an automatic fidelity assessment.

| Italian | English guidance |
| --- | --- |
| attribuzione istituzionale | institutional attribution, or attributed by the named institution; identify who attributes the conduct |
| accertato | established, documented, or found, according to the source and its standard of proof |
| contestato | contested, disputed, alleged or challenged according to whether the context concerns an accusation or disagreement |
| presunto | alleged or presumed, preserving the source's qualification |
| non dimostrato; non implica | not demonstrated / not established; does not imply; retain the scope of the limitation |
| condanna; condanna in primo grado | conviction; conviction at first instance, stating the court and procedural date where known |
| stato del procedimento | identify whether allegations, charges, trial, conviction, appeal or a final judgment are actually reported |

An accusation is not a conviction, and a first-instance conviction is not
necessarily final. Do not add legal status, certainty or causal connections
that the cited source does not establish. See the translation rules in
[AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).
